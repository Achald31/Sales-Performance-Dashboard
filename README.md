# Sales Performance Dashboard | Excel, MySQL & Power BI

## Project Overview

This is my first end-to-end Data Analytics project, where I analyzed a retail sales dataset and developed an interactive Sales Performance Dashboard using Microsoft Excel, MySQL, and Power BI.

The objective was to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights by performing data cleaning, SQL-based analysis, and interactive data visualization.

## Tools & Technologies
- **Microsoft Excel:** Data cleaning and preprocessing
- **MySQL:** Data analysis and SQL queries
- **Power BI:** Dashboard development and visualization
- **DAX:** KPI calculations and business metrics

## Project Workflow

### 1. Data Cleaning & Preprocessing – Excel
- Imported and explored the raw retail sales dataset.
- Removed duplicate records.
- Identified and handled missing values.
- Verified and corrected date formats.
- Prepared the cleaned dataset for SQL analysis.

### 2. Data Analysis – MySQL

Imported the cleaned dataset into MySQL and performed data analysis using SQL queries.

Key business metrics and analysis included:
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Average Sales
- Total Customers
- Profit Margin
- Sales by Category
- Sales by Region
- Sales by Segment
- Profit by State
- Top 10 Customers
- Top 10 Products
- Monthly Sales Trends
- Delivery Time Analysis

### 3. Dashboard Development – Power BI

Connected the cleaned dataset to Power BI and developed an interactive Sales Performance Dashboard.

**Dashboard Features:**
- KPI Cards for key business metrics
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Sales by Category
- Sales by Region
- Sales by Segment
- Top 10 Customers
- Top 10 Products
- Profit by State
- Interactive slicers for Region, Category, Segment, and Year

Used DAX measures to calculate key performance indicators and provide a dynamic reporting experience.

## Key Business Insights

- The Technology category generated the highest sales.
- The West region recorded the highest sales among all regions.
- The Consumer segment contributed the largest share of total sales.
- A relatively small group of customers contributed significantly to overall sales.
- Monthly sales trends showed variations throughout the year.
- Monitoring profit margins alongside sales growth is important for maintaining business profitability.
